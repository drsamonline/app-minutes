The meeting

Type as it happens

One line, one item. The first word decides which list it goes in — so nothing can land in both. No audio is recorded and nothing is transcribed: this structures what you type.

d: a decision (add @Name for who moved it)  ·  a: an action (add @Name for the owner)  ·  topic: starts a topic  ·  n: a note

What it measures

The clock comes from the moments you press “Mark a new topic now”. Pasted minutes carry no clock, and the app does not invent one.
