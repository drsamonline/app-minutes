Decisions are marked

A decision is not the same as a remark. The minutes mark the line where something was settled, so you can find it again without re-reading the whole meeting.

What it refuses

It will not name a speaker it cannot identify, and it will not invent a decision nobody made. An unnamed attendee stays unnamed.
